1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The CP-500 Series centrifugal process pump is designed and manufactured in
accordance with API Standard 610, 12th Edition (Centrifugal Pumps for
Petroleum, Petrochemical, and Natural Gas Industries). These pumps are intended
for continuous service in demanding refinery, chemical plant, and pipeline
applications where reliability and long service life are paramount.

Pump Designation:       CP-500 Series (OH2 / BB2 configurations)
Pump Type:              Single-stage, radially split, overhung centrifugal
Design Standard:        API 610, 12th Edition
Flow Range:             500 to 2,000 GPM (113 to 454 m³/h)
Maximum Head:           800 ft (244 m) at best efficiency point
Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP): 600 PSIG
Temperature Range:      -50°F to +450°F (-46°C to +232°C)
Maximum Speed:          3,600 RPM (60 Hz), 3,000 RPM (50 Hz)
Driver:                 Electric motor, direct-coupled via flexible coupling
Nozzle Loads:           Per API 610 Table 4 (2x standard)
Casing Material:        ASTM A216 WCB carbon steel (standard)
                        ASTM A351 CF8M stainless steel (optional)

The pumps are equipped with heavy-duty bearing housings, robust mechanical
seals, and are designed for a minimum L10 bearing life of 25,000 hours at
maximum continuous rated conditions. All pressure-containing components are
hydrostatically tested to 1.5x MAWP before shipment.

2. COMPONENTS
================================================================================

2.1 Impeller
    - Type: Semi-open (for clean liquids) or closed (for clean and slightly
      contaminated liquids)
    - Number of vanes: 12
    - Material: Stainless steel AISI 316L (standard); Duplex SS 2205 (optional)
    - Impeller diameter (maximum): 14.0 in (355.6 mm)
    - Impeller diameter (minimum): 10.0 in (254.0 mm)
    - Trim capability: 20% maximum diameter reduction
    - Dynamic balance: G2.5 per ISO 1940-1
    - Attachment: Keyed hub with locknut and tab washer

2.2 Volute Casing
    - Type: Single-volute (< 500 GPM), double-volute (≥ 500 GPM)
    - Discharge nozzle orientation: Configurable (top, side, or bottom)
    - Casing split: Radial (perpendicular to shaft axis)
    - Drain plug: 3/4 in NPT at lowest point
    - Vent plug: 1/2 in NPT at highest point
    - Foot-mounted design for ease of alignment and maintenance

2.3 Mechanical Seal
    - Type: John Crane Type 1 single pusher mechanical seal
    - Seal material: Carbon/silicon carbide faces (standard)
                     Silicon carbide/silicon carbide (for abrasive service)
    - Elastomers: Fluoroelastomer (Viton) standard; EPDM optional
    - Gland material: 316 stainless steel
    - Seal flush plan: API Plan 11 (standard), Plan 23 (hot service),
                       Plan 52 (toxic/hazardous service)
    - Expected seal life: 24,000 hours minimum under normal conditions
    - Target leakage: Zero fugitive emissions (VOC < 500 ppm)

2.4 Bearing Housing
    - Radial bearing: SKF 6309 deep groove ball bearing
    - Thrust bearing: SKF 7309 angular contact ball bearing (pair)
    - Bearing housing: Cast iron, precision-bored
    - Lubrication: Oil bath (standard), oil mist (optional)
    - Constant level oiler: Trico model, 8 oz capacity
    - Inboard oil seal: Labyrinth type, non-contacting, carbon steel
    - Outboard oil seal: Labyrinth type with bearing isolator

2.5 Shaft
    - Material: AISI 4140 alloy steel, heat treated to 28-32 HRC
    - Maximum allowable runout (TIR at seal face): 0.002 in (0.05 mm)
    - Maximum allowable runout (TIR at coupling hub): 0.003 in (0.076 mm)
    - Shaft deflection at seal face (API 610 limit): 0.002 in maximum
    - Surface finish at seal area: 32 RMS or better
    - Surface finish at bearing seats: 16 RMS or better
    - Keyway: Parallel key, 3/8 x 3/16 in (standard size)

2.6 Wear Rings
    - Location: Casing and impeller wear rings (both)
    - Material: 316 stainless steel (standard service)
                Chrome oxide coated SS (abrasive service)
    - Diametral clearance (new): 0.010-0.015 in (per pump size)
    - Maximum allowable clearance before replacement: 0.020 in
    - Anti-rotation: Lock pins prevent rotation in casing bore

2.7 Stuffing Box
    - Provides housing for the mechanical seal
    - Bore tolerance: +0.000/-0.001 in
    - Flush connection: 1/4 in NPT (API Plan 11 flush)
    - Cooling jacket (optional for high-temperature service)

3. SEAL TYPES
================================================================================

3.1 Mechanical Seal (Preferred Installation)
    Mechanical seals are the preferred sealing arrangement for all process pump
    applications. They provide essentially zero leakage to atmosphere, complying
    with EPA 40 CFR Part 63 (MACT) and API 682 fugitive emission standards.

    Installation requirements:
    - Shaft runout must be <0.002 in TIR before seal installation
    - Stuffing box face must be perpendicular to shaft within 0.002 in TIR
    - Seal setting dimension: Per manufacturer's installation drawing
    - Hydraulic pressure test of gland and flush connections before startup
    - Break-in period: Reduce speed by 20% for first 4 hours of operation

    Expected leakage rate: 0 drops per minute (zero visible leakage)
    If visible leakage occurs within the first 24 hours, shut down and reseat
    seal faces.

3.2 Gland Packing (Permitted for Water and Non-Hazardous Fluids)
    Gland packing is permitted as an alternative sealing method for clear water
    service and other non-hazardous, non-toxic process fluids where fugitive
    emission limits are not applicable.

    Packing specification: Braided PTFE (standard water service)
                           Graphite-impregnated (high temperature >200°F)
    Number of packing rings: 5 rings
    Maximum allowable leakage: 10 drops per minute (approximately 3 mL/min)
    If leakage exceeds 10 drops/min, tighten gland nuts 1/6 turn and monitor.
    Do not overtighten packing; this causes shaft sleeve wear and overheating.
    Replace packing set annually, or when leakage cannot be controlled.

5. VIBRATION STANDARDS (ISO 10816 / ISO 20816)
================================================================================

Vibration measurements per ISO 10816-3 for industrial machines with power above
15 kW. Measurements taken at bearing housing locations in three directions
(radial-horizontal, radial-vertical, axial). Units: mm/s RMS (velocity).

Classification for CP-500 pumps: Class II (machine power 15 kW to 75 kW)

NORMAL:   Less than 2.3 mm/s RMS
  Pump is operating within acceptable vibration limits. Continue normal
  operation. Log vibration readings at each monthly inspection.

WARNING:  2.3 mm/s to 4.5 mm/s RMS (Zone B/C boundary)
  Elevated vibration has been detected. The pump may continue to operate, but
  a detailed investigation must be initiated within 5 business days.
  Perform the following: Check pump-to-motor alignment (accept <0.002 in TIR
  offset, <0.001 in/in angularity). Check impeller balance. Check bearing
  condition via vibration spectrum analysis. Check suction and discharge
  piping supports for resonance. Reduce flow rate if cavitation is suspected.

CRITICAL: Greater than 4.5 mm/s RMS (Zone D)
  Pump vibration is at a level that is liable to cause damage to the machine.
  Initiate orderly pump shutdown immediately. Transfer service to spare pump
  if available. Do not restart pump until root cause has been identified and
  corrective action taken. Bearing replacement, re-alignment, or impeller
  replacement is required.

6. CAVITATION DETECTION AND DIAGNOSIS
================================================================================

6.1 Signs of Cavitation
    - Audible noise: Crackling or popping sound resembling gravel in the pump
    - Increased vibration levels: Typically broadband increase in spectrum
    - Performance degradation: Reduced head (>3% drop from rated curve) and
      reduced flow rate at the same operating point
    - Pitting damage: Visible erosion on impeller vanes (suction surface) and
      casing surfaces when disassembled for inspection

6.2 Common Causes of Cavitation
    - Insufficient Net Positive Suction Head Available (NPSHa):
      NPSHa must exceed NPSHr (required) by minimum 0.5 ft margin
    - High liquid temperature reducing vapor pressure margin
    - Excessive flow rate (operating far right of pump curve)
    - Blocked or undersized suction piping
    - Air entrainment in suction piping
    - Vortexing at suction sump (insufficient submergence)

6.3 Remedies for Cavitation
    - Increase suction pressure by raising suction vessel liquid level
    - Reduce pump speed (if variable frequency drive is installed)
    - Trim impeller diameter to reduce NPSHr
    - Check suction strainer for blockage; clean if differential pressure >2 psi
    - Install suction line booster pump
    - Reduce liquid temperature by improving heat exchanger performance
    - Add cavitation-suppression vortex breaker to suction sump

7. IMPELLER INSPECTION
================================================================================

7.1 Wear Ring Clearance Check
    Measure diametral clearance between impeller wear ring and casing wear ring
    using feeler gauges or inside/outside micrometers.
    - New clearance: 0.010 to 0.015 in
    - Maximum allowable clearance: 0.020 in
    - If clearance exceeds 0.020 in: Replace both wear rings (impeller and casing)

7.2 Vane Erosion Assessment
    Inspect all 12 vane surfaces for erosion, cavitation pitting, and mechanical
    damage. Any vane eroded more than 10% of its original thickness (measured
    at mid-span) requires impeller replacement. Blend repair is not permitted
    on impeller vanes due to balance criticality.

7.3 Impeller Balancing Procedure
    After any impeller repair, trim, or replacement, perform dynamic balance:
    - Mount impeller on balance arbor
    - Balance to ISO 1940-1 Grade G2.5
    - At 3,600 RPM: maximum residual imbalance = 0.5 oz-in per plane
    - Correction method: Material removal from balance land on hub face
    - Re-check balance after correction; maximum 3 correction iterations

8. LUBRICATION SCHEDULE
================================================================================

Weekly Inspection:
    - Check oil level in bearing housing via sight glass
    - Top up with ISO VG 68 oil if level below center of sight glass
    - Check for evidence of oil discoloration (milky appearance = water ingress)
    - Check for oil leakage from seals and drain plugs

Monthly Inspection:
    - Perform oil analysis sample (30 mL sample from drain port)
    - Submit to oil analysis laboratory for: viscosity (40°C), water content,
      particle count, metals analysis (Fe, Cr, Cu, Al, Pb, Sn)
    - Acceptance criteria for continued use: viscosity ±10% of nominal,
      water <0.05%, ISO cleanliness 16/14/11 or better
    - Check coupling condition: inspect for wear, cracking, and alignment

Annual Inspection:
    - Drain and flush bearing housing with flushing oil
    - Refill with fresh ISO VG 68 oil
    - If oil analysis shows bearing metal contamination (Fe >200 ppm or
      Cu >100 ppm): replace bearings regardless of hours run
    - Replace bearing housing lip seals and labyrinth isolators
    - Check shaft runout; align pump/motor if runout >0.002 in

9. FAULT CODES AND CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
================================================================================

CODE | FAULT DESCRIPTION          | SEVERITY | RECOMMENDED ACTION                      | RESP TIME
-----|----------------------------|----------|-----------------------------------------|----------
P001 | Cavitation                 | HIGH     | Check NPSH, reduce flow                 | 4h
P002 | Seal leakage               | MEDIUM   | Replace mechanical seal                 | 8h
P003 | Bearing overheating        | HIGH     | Check lubrication, replace bearing      | 6h
P004 | Impeller wear              | MEDIUM   | Measure clearance, replace if >0.020 in | 16h
P005 | Vibration high             | HIGH     | Balance impeller, check alignment       | 8h
P006 | Flow rate drop             | MEDIUM   | Check wear rings, clear blockage        | 12h
P007 | Shaft seal leak            | HIGH     | Replace shaft seal                      | 8h
P008 | Motor overload             | HIGH     | Check impeller for blockage             | 4h
P009 | Suction strainer blocked   | MEDIUM   | Clean strainer                          | 2h
P010 | Discharge pressure low     | MEDIUM   | Check impeller, wear rings              | 8h
P011 | Oil contamination          | HIGH     | Flush and replace oil, find source      | 6h
P012 | Coupling misalignment      | MEDIUM   | Realign pump/motor                      | 4h
P013 | Shaft bow                  | HIGH     | Replace shaft                           | 24h
P014 | Casing crack               | CRITICAL | Immediate shutdown, replace casing      | 2h
P015 | Bearing housing crack      | CRITICAL | Immediate shutdown                      | 2h

FAULT CODE DETAILS:

P001 - Cavitation
  Trigger: Operator reports cracking or popping noise. Vibration elevated
  >3.0 mm/s RMS. Flow rate reduced >5% from design point at same speed.
  Action: Calculate NPSHa. Check suction strainer pressure drop. Reduce flow
  by partially closing discharge valve. If NPSHa < NPSHr + 0.5 ft margin,
  halt pump and investigate suction system.

P003 - Bearing Overheating
  Trigger: Bearing housing temperature >85°C (measured by contact thermometer
  or infrared pyrometer). Normal bearing temperature: 40-70°C above ambient.
  Action: Check oil level immediately. If oil level is acceptable, take oil
  sample for analysis. If bearing temperature continues to rise, shut down pump.
  Replace bearing set and flush housing before restart.

P014 - Casing Crack
  Trigger: Visible crack in pump casing. Liquid leakage at casing joint.
  Abnormal pressure reading or sudden drop in pump performance.
  Action: Immediate controlled shutdown. Isolate pump from process using
  block valves. Depressurize and drain casing. Do not attempt repair in situ;
  replace casing with new or remanufactured unit. Investigate root cause
  (pressure surge, waterhammer, thermal shock, or manufacturing defect).
