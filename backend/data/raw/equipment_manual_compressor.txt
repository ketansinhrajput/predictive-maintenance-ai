1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The RC-500 Series reciprocating compressor is a heavy-duty, two-stage,
double-acting industrial compressor designed for continuous-duty gas compression
in refinery, petrochemical, natural gas processing, and industrial utility
applications. The compressor is designed and manufactured in compliance with
API Standard 618 (Reciprocating Compressors for Petroleum, Chemical, and Gas
Industry Services), 5th Edition.

Compressor Designation:     RC-500 Series
Compressor Type:            Two-stage, double-acting, horizontal balanced-opposed
Driver Power:               500 HP (373 kW) at 1,200 RPM
Rated Capacity:             1,000 CFM (28.3 m³/min) at 250 PSIG discharge
Inlet Conditions:           14.7 PSIA, 60°F (15.6°C) gas at standard conditions
Stage 1 Suction Pressure:   14.7 PSIA (atmospheric)
Stage 1 Discharge Pressure: 62 PSIG (interstage)
Stage 2 Suction Pressure:   58 PSIG (after intercooler)
Stage 2 Discharge Pressure: 250 PSIG
Compression Ratio (Stage 1): 5.2:1
Compression Ratio (Stage 2): 4.9:1
Interstage Temperature:     320°F (160°C) maximum
Final Discharge Temperature: 300°F (149°C) maximum
Frame Size:                 RC-500 (medium frame)
Stroke:                     8.0 in (203 mm)
Speed Range:                800 to 1,200 RPM
Cooling Method:             Water-cooled cylinders and intercooler
Design Gas:                 Air (standard); process gas variants available
Lubrication:                Force-feed splash lubrication system (frame)
                            Cylinder lubrication: Divider block force-feed

The balanced-opposed configuration positions cylinders on opposite sides of
the crankshaft, minimizing unbalanced inertia forces and reducing frame vibration.
This design is preferred for API 618 applications requiring long, trouble-free
service life with minimal foundation loading.

2. COMPONENTS
================================================================================

2.1 Cylinder Assemblies (2 Stages)
    - Stage 1 cylinder bore: 12.0 in (304.8 mm)
    - Stage 2 cylinder bore: 7.5 in (190.5 mm)
    - Cylinder material: Cast iron (stage 1); ductile iron (stage 2)
    - Cylinder liner: Replaceable cast iron liner in both stages
    - Cylinder cooling: Water jacket with inlet/outlet temperature monitoring
    - Cylinder cooling water flow rate: 15 GPM minimum per cylinder
    - Valve ports: 2 suction, 2 discharge per cylinder end (double-acting)
    - Cylinder lubrication: Divider-block injector, 10 injection points

2.2 Pistons and Rings
    - Piston material: Cast iron (stage 1); steel (stage 2)
    - Piston rings: PTFE-filled (15% glass fiber) for oil-free sealing
    - Number of piston rings: 4 per piston end (compression rings)
    - Rider rings: 2 per piston (support rings, PTFE)
    - Maximum allowable ring gap (end gap): 0.018 in (0.46 mm)
    - New ring end gap: 0.006 to 0.010 in
    - Maximum allowable blowby: 5% of theoretical cylinder capacity
    - Piston rod: Alloy steel, chrome-plated sealing surface
    - Piston rod diameter: 3.0 in (Stage 1), 2.5 in (Stage 2)
    - Rod packing: PTFE chevron packing, 5 rings per stuffing box

2.3 Connecting Rods
    - Material: Forged alloy steel AISI 4340
    - Big-end bearing: Insert-type, babbitt-lined (tin-based white metal)
    - Small-end bearing: Bronze bushing
    - Connecting rod bolt torque: 950 ft-lb (Stage 1), 780 ft-lb (Stage 2)
    - Bearing clearance (new): 0.003-0.005 in
    - Maximum allowable bearing clearance: 0.010 in before replacement

2.4 Crankshaft
    - Material: Forged alloy steel, ASTM A668 Class D
    - Number of throws: 4 (2 per stage, horizontally opposed)
    - Main bearing journals: 5 (three main, two outboard)
    - Main bearing material: Babbitt-lined insert bearings
    - Crank pin journal diameter: 5.0 in (Stage 1), 4.5 in (Stage 2)
    - Main journal diameter: 5.5 in
    - Maximum journal taper: 0.001 in per inch of length
    - Maximum journal out-of-round: 0.001 in
    - Crankshaft end float: 0.008 to 0.014 in

2.5 Crossheads
    - Material: Cast iron with babbitt-lined shoes
    - Crosshead pin: Alloy steel, induction hardened
    - Crosshead clearance (top/bottom): 0.008-0.012 in
    - Maximum allowable clearance: 0.020 in
    - Crosshead guide: Integral with frame, water-cooled
    - Lubrication: Pressure-fed through crosshead pin

2.6 Valve Assemblies (Plate Valves)
    - Type: Concentric ring plate valve (suction and discharge)
    - Valve material: Stainless steel 17-4PH plate; PEEK guard rings
    - Valve springs: Stainless steel 316L
    - Lift (valve travel): 0.060 in (Stage 1), 0.050 in (Stage 2)
    - Number of suction valves: 4 per cylinder (2 per end)
    - Number of discharge valves: 4 per cylinder (2 per end)
    - Valve unloaders: Finger-type pneumatic unloaders on suction valves
    - Seat flatness tolerance: Maximum 0.002 in across full seat surface

2.7 Intercooler
    - Type: Shell-and-tube heat exchanger, fixed tube sheet
    - Shell material: Carbon steel ASME SA-516-70
    - Tube material: Admiralty brass (70% Cu, 29% Zn, 1% Sn)
    - Number of tubes: 96 (3/4 in OD, 16 BWG wall)
    - Tube length: 72 in (1,830 mm)
    - Cooling water side: Shell side (2-pass)
    - Gas side: Tube side (1-pass)
    - Design pressure (gas side): 100 PSIG
    - Design pressure (water side): 150 PSIG
    - Cooling water flow: 30 GPM at 85°F inlet temperature
    - Design approach temperature: 20°F (interstage gas to cooling water)
    - Maximum pressure drop (gas side, clean): 5 psi

2.8 Frame and Crankcase
    - Material: Grey cast iron, stress-relieved
    - Bedplate: Fabricated steel, grouted to concrete foundation
    - Foundation bolts: 1-1/4 in diameter, epoxy grouted
    - Oil sump capacity: 25 gallons (94.6 liters)
    - Oil pump: Gear-type, engine-driven, 10 GPM capacity
    - Oil filtration: Full-flow spin-on filter, 25-micron nominal

3. VALVE MAINTENANCE
================================================================================

Valve condition is the most critical maintenance item on a reciprocating
compressor. Faulty valves cause inefficiency, overheating, and potential
compressor failure if not addressed promptly.

Inspection Interval: Every 4,000 operating hours (approximately every 6 months
at continuous duty). Valves operating on hydrogen or other light gases should
be inspected every 2,000 hours.

Inspection Procedure:
  1. Depressurize and isolate compressor. Allow cylinders to cool below 120°F.
  2. Remove valve covers and withdraw valve assemblies using valve puller tool.
  3. Disassemble each valve into plate(s), guard, springs, and seat.
  4. Clean all components with approved solvent (MEK or isopropyl alcohol).
  5. Inspect valve plate for cracks, warping, and wear. Measure plate thickness;
     replace if worn more than 20% of original thickness (nominal: 0.090 in).
  6. Check valve springs for set and corrosion; replace if free length is <90%
     of new length (nominal: 0.75 in free length).
  7. Check seat flatness using precision optical flat and monochromatic light.
     Maximum allowable deviation: 0.002 in across full seat contact surface.
  8. Lap seat if deviation is 0.001-0.002 in; replace seat if >0.002 in.
  9. Reassemble valve and perform hydrostatic leak test at 1.5x working pressure.
  10. Install valve with new O-ring and torque valve cap bolts to spec (85 ft-lb).

4. PISTON RING REPLACEMENT
================================================================================

Inspection Interval: Every 8,000 operating hours or annually, whichever occurs
first. Early replacement is indicated by excessive blowby (>5%) or gas leakage
detected by elevated interstage temperature.

Ring Condition Criteria:
  - Measure ring end gap using feeler gauge inserted into cylinder bore
  - New ring end gap: 0.006 to 0.010 in
  - Replace if end gap exceeds 0.018 in (maximum allowable)
  - Check ring groove clearance (side clearance): maximum 0.004 in
  - Inspect ring seating surface for scoring and glazing

Replacement Procedure:
  Step 1: Depressurize and isolate cylinder. Drain cylinder lubrication lines.
  Step 2: Remove cylinder head and piston rod packing gland.
  Step 3: Withdraw piston assembly from cylinder using piston removal sling.
  Step 4: Remove worn rings using ring expander tool. Do not use screwdrivers
          or sharp tools that may damage piston grooves.
  Step 5: Clean piston grooves with solvent. Inspect grooves for wear and
          deformation. Measure groove width; replace piston if groove width
          exceeds new ring width by more than 0.003 in.
  Step 6: Install new rings with gaps staggered at 120° intervals around the
          piston circumference to minimize blowby.
  Step 7: Check ring-to-groove clearance after installation (0.001-0.003 in).
  Step 8: Re-install piston with new O-rings and gaskets.
  Step 9: Perform leak check. Monitor interstage temperature for 24 hours.

5. INTERCOOLER CLEANING
================================================================================

Annual tube bundle cleaning is required to maintain thermal performance and
prevent fouling-induced pressure drop increases.

Cleaning Procedure:
  1. Isolate cooling water and gas sides using block valves.
  2. Drain both sides and purge gas side with nitrogen.
  3. Remove channel covers on both ends of the heat exchanger.
  4. Inspect tube ends for pitting, erosion, and fouling deposits.
  5. Prepare 10% citric acid solution (100 g citric acid per 1,000 mL water).
  6. Circulate citric acid solution through tubes at 2 GPM for 4 hours at 120°F.
  7. Flush tubes with clean fresh water until pH of effluent is neutral (pH 7).
  8. Inspect individual tubes with tube brush and borescope.
  9. Plug any tubes showing through-wall pitting or leakage (max 10% plugged).
  10. Reinstall channel covers with new gaskets. Hydrostatically test at
      1.5x design pressure before returning to service.

Performance Acceptance:
  Pressure drop (gas side) must not exceed 5 psi when operating at rated flow
  and clean condition. Pressure drop >5 psi indicates fouling and requires
  immediate cleaning. Pressure drop >8 psi indicates severe fouling and
  possible tube blockage; inspect individual tubes.

6. PRESSURE SAFETY VALVES
================================================================================

PSV1 — Stage 1 Discharge Safety Valve
  - Set pressure: 175 PSIG (1.21 MPa gauge)
  - Full open pressure: 193 PSIG (set + 10%)
  - Reseat pressure: 166 PSIG (set - 5%)
  - Orifice: 0.785 in² (D orifice)
  - Body material: Cast iron; trim material 316 SS
  - Test interval: Annually (or after any overpressure event)

PSV2 — Stage 2 Discharge Safety Valve
  - Set pressure: 275 PSIG (1.90 MPa gauge)
  - Full open pressure: 303 PSIG (set + 10%)
  - Reseat pressure: 261 PSIG (set - 5%)
  - Orifice: 0.503 in² (C orifice)
  - Body material: Cast iron; trim material 316 SS
  - Test interval: Annually (or after any overpressure event)

Testing Procedure:
  Use calibrated test stand per API 527. Apply increasing test pressure until
  valve opens. Verify full-open pressure is within +3% / -0% of set pressure.
  Verify valve reseats cleanly without simmering. If valve fails to open within
  +10% of set pressure, or fails to reseat tightly, remove from service and
  refurbish or replace. Document test date and results; update valve tag.

7. LUBRICATION OIL ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Oil analysis is a critical condition monitoring tool for reciprocating compressors.
Sample frequency: Every 1,000 operating hours (approximately every 6 weeks).

Sampling Procedure:
  - Take sample from running compressor at sampling port on main oil header
  - Use clean sampling syringe; flush sampling port before taking sample
  - Submit 100 mL sample to approved laboratory within 48 hours of sampling

Acceptance Criteria:
  Viscosity (at 40°C):     Acceptable range: ISO VG 68 ±10% (61.2-74.8 cSt)
                            Out of spec indicates oil degradation or dilution
  Water content:           Maximum 0.1% by weight (Karl Fischer method)
                            Above 0.1% indicates cooling water in-leakage
  Total Acid Number (TAN): Maximum 2.0 mg KOH/g (new oil: 0.5 mg KOH/g)
  Particle count:          Maximum ISO 16/14/11 (per ISO 4406)
  Iron (Fe):               < 100 ppm (crankshaft bearing wear indicator)
  Copper (Cu):             < 50 ppm (crosshead pin bushing wear indicator)
  Lead (Pb):               < 30 ppm (babbitt bearing wear indicator)
  Silicon (Si):            < 10 ppm (airborne contamination / ingestion)

Action on Out-of-Spec Results:
  If any parameter exceeds maximum: drain oil, flush frame with flushing oil,
  refill with fresh ISO VG 68 oil, and re-sample after 100 hours. If particle
  count exceeds limit, inspect oil filter bypass valve and replace filter element.
  If water content exceeds limit, inspect intercooler for tube-to-shell leakage.

8. FAULT CODES AND CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
================================================================================

CODE | FAULT DESCRIPTION           | SEVERITY | RECOMMENDED ACTION                        | RESP TIME
-----|-----------------------------|----------|-------------------------------------------|----------
C001 | Valve failure - suction     | HIGH     | Replace suction valve plates              | 8h
C002 | Valve failure - discharge   | HIGH     | Replace discharge valve plates            | 8h
C003 | Piston ring wear            | MEDIUM   | Replace piston rings                      | 16h
C004 | Cylinder overheating        | CRITICAL | Reduce load, check cooling water          | 2h
C005 | Intercooler fouling         | MEDIUM   | Clean tube bundle                         | 12h
C006 | Oil pressure low            | CRITICAL | Check oil pump, stop unit                 | 1h
C007 | High vibration              | HIGH     | Check balance, bearing wear               | 8h
C008 | Crosshead wear              | HIGH     | Replace crosshead shoes                   | 24h
C009 | Rod packing leak            | MEDIUM   | Replace rod packing                       | 8h
C010 | Crankshaft bearing failure  | CRITICAL | Immediate shutdown                        | 2h
C011 | High discharge temperature  | HIGH     | Check cooler, reduce compression ratio    | 6h
C012 | Low inlet pressure          | MEDIUM   | Check inlet filter, suction line          | 4h
C013 | PSV lift                    | HIGH     | Reduce pressure, inspect system           | 4h
C014 | Intercooler leak            | HIGH     | Repair tube bundle                        | 8h
C015 | Lube oil contamination      | HIGH     | Flush system, find contamination source   | 8h

FAULT CODE DETAILS:

C001 / C002 - Valve Failure (Suction / Discharge)
  Trigger: Elevated discharge temperature (>10% above normal), reduced capacity,
  abnormal valve temperature on cylinder temperature monitoring, or audible
  valve clatter.
  Diagnosis: Thermal imaging of valve covers; discharge temperature analysis;
  cylinder pressure-volume (PV) card analysis using compressor analyzer.
  Action: Shutdown affected stage. Remove and inspect all valve assemblies.
  Replace plates and springs. Check seat for scoring. Re-test before restart.

C004 - Cylinder Overheating
  Trigger: Cylinder jacket water outlet temperature >180°F (82°C), or
  discharge temperature >320°F (160°C) for Stage 1, or >300°F (149°C) for Stage 2.
  Causes: Loss of cooling water flow, valve failure causing high compression ratio,
  piston ring failure causing gas blowby recirculation.
  Action: Reduce compressor load to minimum. Verify cooling water flow rate
  (minimum 15 GPM per cylinder). Inspect for valve failure and ring condition.
  Do not restart until temperature drops below 150°F (66°C).

C006 - Oil Pressure Low
  Trigger: Frame oil pressure drops below 25 PSIG (normal: 45-60 PSIG).
  CRITICAL — Continued operation with low oil pressure will result in
  catastrophic crankshaft bearing failure within minutes.
  Action: Immediate shutdown (ESD system will activate automatically).
  Investigate: check oil level (minimum 1/2 sight glass), check oil pump
  drive coupling, check pressure relief valve, check oil filter differential
  pressure. Do not restart until cause is identified and corrected.

C010 - Crankshaft Bearing Failure
  Trigger: High vibration on frame (>10 mm/s RMS), metal particles in oil
  (Fe >500 ppm), audible knock from crankcase, or bearing temperature alarm.
  Action: Immediate emergency shutdown. Do not restart. Open crankcase doors
  and inspect all main and rod bearings. Measure bearing clearances. Replace
  all bearings showing signs of wiping, scoring, or seizure. Inspect crankshaft
  journals for damage; re-grind or replace crankshaft as required.
